# Empire Restoration Estimator: Phone Installation

Use the HTTPS address shown in the Azure Static Web App after deployment.

## Android with Microsoft Edge

1. Open the Azure-hosted estimator address in Microsoft Edge.
2. Sign in if your deployment is configured to require authentication.
3. Tap **Install App** in the estimator header. If the button is not shown, open the Edge menu and choose **Add to phone** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm installation.
5. Launch **Empire Estimator** from the home screen.
6. Open the app once while online so the offline app shell is cached.

## Android with Google Chrome

1. Open the Azure-hosted estimator address in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm installation.

## iPhone or iPad with Safari

1. Open the Azure-hosted estimator address in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name **Empire Estimator** and tap **Add**.
5. Launch from the home screen and open the app once while online.

## Job-file workflow on phones

- Use **Save Job File** to download the `.ertjob` record.
- Use **Open Job File** to reopen a saved record.
- Use **Export History CSV** regularly as a backup.
- Drafts and Job History remain local to the specific browser installation unless cloud synchronization is added separately.
